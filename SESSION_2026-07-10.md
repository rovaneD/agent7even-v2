## Bug fixed

**Owner guard bypass for stale team member profile flags**

- `lib/teamPermissions.ts` returned `isOwner: true` when `profiles.is_account_owner !== false` before consulting active `team_members` membership.
- Other workspace resolution paths already treat active `team_members` rows as the source of truth and can backfill stale profile rows.
- Concrete trigger: an active team member with a stale/null `profiles.is_account_owner` could pass `requireWorkspaceOwner`, then mutate owner-scoped resources through routes such as Zernio connect/reconnect, GA connect/disconnect, or approval/rejection endpoints.

**Fix:** Active `team_members` membership now overrides stale profile flags before owner permissions are granted. Missing profiles fail closed with minimal member permissions, and stale active member profile rows are backfilled to `is_account_owner=false`.
