## 1. The locked decision

> **Maya is the interface. The OS is the substance. Which one leads depends on whether the user has signed up yet.**

- **Marketing site (pre-signup): OS-forward.** Lead with the system and the outcome — "marketing gets done without becoming your job" — backed by the real agent architecture (12 agents, autonomous/approval split) and the approval framework. Maya is named one line under the headline as the director / intelligence layer coordinating the specialists.
- **Logged-in product (post-signup): Maya-forward.** Chat is the front door; the OS is what Maya operates. Matches locked product context.

This is not a compromise. It is the actual architecture: the OS is what you sell, Maya is how you operate it, and a cold visitor vs. a logged-in user sit at different points in that relationship.

---

## 2. Why this resolves the audit collision

Both auditors were right about different surfaces:
- Marketing auditor (saw only the site): "demote Maya, lead with the OS… people don't buy Siri, they buy the ecosystem."
- Logged-in auditor (saw the live product): "Primary: Maya Chat. Secondary: Marketing OS. Honestly, the right decision."

They describe two surfaces, not one contradiction. Pre-signup leads with the machine; post-signup leads with the operator.

## 5. Two-ICP resolution

The product serves two buyers; messaging must stop telling one story four times (Use Cases audit, scored 6.0).

- **Primary ICP:** survival-driven solo / near-solo SMB (coffee shop, HVAC, dentist, salon, gym) — lacks time, marketing literacy, consistency, affordable options.
- **Equally important:** developed-brand / agency / creator / operator — media-heavy, sophistication-tolerant.

**Use Cases rebuild differentiates by which agents matter to whom, not by stock photo:**
- Local service → reviews-roadmap aside, SEO scan, promotions, competitor reports.
- Ecommerce → launches, content cadence, competitor reports, media generation (heavy).
- Creators → content consistency, launches, audience.
- Agencies → multi-client (note: tier/packaging gap flagged in pricing; not solved here).

**Tier alignment:** the ProAgent-only premium-media gate (from A3) maps to this split — survival-case SMB lives on Starter; media-heavy operator climbs to ProAgent for premium image/video. The upgrade path mirrors the ICP split.
