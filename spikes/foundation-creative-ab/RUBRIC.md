# Judging rubric (decided BEFORE viewing outputs)

Per item, score honestly:

1. **On-brand for Maya / Agent7even?** — Would this read as our product speaking to our ICP, not generic SaaS?
2. **Premium — would we publish it?** — Not "AI-looking" or template marketplace quality.
3. **Arm A vs Arm B** — Is the Foundation-grounded brief visibly better than the shallow prompt? (Not just different — better.)
4. **Image only:** Text rendered correctly and legibly (if brief requested text)?
5. **Video only:** Does it feel like *this* business's reel, or generic AI UGC anyone could use? (Slick ≠ on-brand.)

**Separate verdicts:**
- **IMAGE:** GO only if Arm A beats B on most items AND we'd publish at least one Arm A output.
- **VIDEO:** GO only if Arm A beats B on most items AND at least one clip feels on-brand for Agent7even.

A NO-GO on either deliverable is valid and valuable.
