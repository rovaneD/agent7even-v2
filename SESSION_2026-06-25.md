### Product trace (YouTube — June 25 audit)

Marketing lists YouTube. App code **does not block** YouTube connect or publish:

- Connect: `PostsClient` + `AnalyticsClient` include `youtube`; `/api/integrations/zernio/connect` passes slug to `getConnectUrl`.
- Publish: `PLATFORM_CAPTION_LIMITS` includes `youtube: 5000`; `createPost` forwards platforms to Zernio with no allowlist in `publisher.ts`.
- **Not** in `ZERNIO_HEADLESS_PLATFORMS` (`facebook`, `instagram`, `threads` only) — YouTube uses standard Zernio OAuth redirect.

Live OAuth/post success still depends on Zernio plan/API — not verified live in this session.
